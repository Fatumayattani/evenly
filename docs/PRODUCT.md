# Product definition

## Promise

**When money comes in, everything important moves closer to ready.**

## Core spaces

### Life
A private financial workspace for income, personal liquidity, bills, selected shared household expenses, emergency protection, taxes, and goals.

### Groups
A configurable workspace for people who contribute toward a shared outcome. The product uses universal language rather than culturally specific labels.

A group can support:
- shared savings
- rotating payouts
- emergency support
- family support
- expense pools
- investment recordkeeping
- custom contribution plans

## Privacy rule

**Private personal finances. Transparent shared commitments. Fully auditable shared money.**

A group sees a member's agreed contribution, amount paid, amount outstanding, and shared treasury activity. It does not see salary, clients, personal balances, spending, taxes, or unrelated groups.

## MVP loop

1. User receives income.
2. Allocation rules split the amount into Life buckets.
3. Upcoming obligations become Ready, Building, or At risk.
4. The user's Group reserve prepares the next agreed contribution.
5. The user contributes when ready.
6. Shared treasury actions require the configured approvals.
7. A user may record a compact state hash on Solana as a verifiable receipt.
